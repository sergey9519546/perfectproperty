# Perfect Property Motion Identity

## Style Prompt

Institutional real-estate intelligence presented as a restrained cinematic command surface: deep cartographic navy, precise amber opportunity signals, cool steel-blue evidence rails, and sparse emerald confirmation states. Motion should feel calibrated and consequential—confident directional pushes, controlled focus pulls, measured data builds, and quiet ambient map movement rather than promotional spectacle.

## Colors

- `#01070c` — primary canvas
- `#061018` — product surface
- `#091721` — raised product surface
- `#f3f6f8` — primary text
- `#9ba9b4` — supporting text
- `#efaa2d` — opportunity and primary action
- `#05d680` — verified/live confirmation
- `#7893a5` — cartographic borders and evidence rails

## Typography

- Geist — statements, navigation, explanatory copy, and product chrome
- Geist Mono — scores, dates, source labels, and calibrated data
- Display statements use weights 800–900; supporting copy uses 300–400; data uses tabular numerals.

## Motion

- Medium-energy, premium technical pacing.
- Primary transition: directional push, 0.45–0.55 seconds, `power3.inOut`.
- Accent transition: focus pull into the closing action.
- Entrances lead with hierarchy and vary direction, duration, and easing.
- Ambient motion stays slow and finite: map drift, scanning rules, and restrained signal pulses.

## What NOT to Do

- No neon cyberpunk palette, purple gradients, or generic blue SaaS glow.
- No elastic/bouncy motion, spinning icons, confetti, or playful overshoot.
- No rounded card-grid montage or decorative fake metrics unrelated to the product.
- No black-box AI imagery, floating robots, or invented product claims.
- No heavy color wash over product UI; preserve crisp cartographic contrast.
